# Neodym Profile

Neodym helps businesses adopt practical AI systems that create measurable operational or commercial gains. It prioritizes production-ready AI over speculative demos, with emphasis on ROI, affordability, fast delivery, and reliable implementation.

Neodym cares most about:

- AI agents and workflow automation.
- Production-ready LLM applications.
- Model capabilities that affect real-world business use.
- AI infrastructure, deployment, evaluation, monitoring, and reliability.
- Cost-efficient inference, hosting, and AI delivery.
- Enterprise AI adoption, pricing, compliance, vendor strategy, and market demand.

High relevance:

- Impacts how companies build, deploy, evaluate, or buy AI systems.
- Creates practical automation or consulting opportunities.
- Changes model, tooling, infrastructure, or vendor choices for client delivery.
- Improves ROI, reliability, affordability, security, compliance, or time-to-value.
- Signals meaningful shifts from major AI ecosystem players or developer platforms.

Lower relevance:

- Pure hype without practical operational impact.
- Academic research with no near-term implementation path.
- Consumer-only updates unless they signal a broader workflow, agent, model, or platform shift.
- Funding/news items unless they affect competitive positioning, partner ecosystems, or client demand.
